    How to play
    
  
      
    
    1. You get 7 questions. Each has lots of right answers, from famous mathematicians to prime numbers.
      2. 
    
    2. Read the question, then you have 20 seconds to name one answer. Wrong guesses just let you try again.
      3. 
    
    3. Any right answer scores, but rarer answers score more. The obvious pick barely moves you; the obscure one sends you far into the tail.
      4. 
    
    4. Watch out for Clichés: the "clever" answer that everyone thinks is clever.
      5. 
    
    5. Your score moves you along the bell curve. Finish +4σ from the mean and you're a true Outlier.
      6. 
    
    6. The Daily is the same for everyone and resets at midnight UK time. Unlimited never runs out.
      7. 
  
    
  
    Tiers
    
  
      
  
      Number questions accept sums, so 2^10, 7*13 or 2^31-1 all work. Spelling mistakes in word answers are forgiven.
